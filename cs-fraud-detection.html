Fraud Detection Platform for Top Credit Card Issuer

$6M engagement · Billions of transactions monitored annually

Situation

A top U.S. credit card issuer needed an AI-powered fraud detection platform capable of monitoring billions of transactions in real time. The engagement involved significant technical complexity, a large delivery team, and a contract structure that created meaningful commercial risk — including convenience termination clauses that could have left the project exposed mid-delivery.

Approach

I closed the $6M engagement and took on accountability for both the commercial and delivery sides. On the delivery side, I managed a 10-person team through implementation — coordinating across technical workstreams, managing client stakeholders, and keeping the program on track through the complexity that comes with large-scale financial services deployments.

On the commercial side, I restructured the contract to shift the risk profile — moving to milestone-based payment terms that improved cash flow predictability and eliminating the convenience termination clauses that had created the most significant exposure. The restructuring protected both the engagement economics and the client relationship.

Impact

The engagement delivered a fraud detection platform now monitoring billions of transactions annually. The contract restructuring eliminated key commercial risk while maintaining a strong client relationship — and the delivery model held up under the operational demands of a major financial institution.
