DataRobot · Partnership

BCG Joint Solution Motion

$500K revenue · $2M pipeline generated

Situation

DataRobot had strong platform capability but limited access to the enterprise AI transformation conversations that start at the strategy level — the conversations where budgets get shaped and platforms get selected. BCG had exactly that access: the client relationships, the transformation credibility, and the mandate to recommend technology. The opportunity was to build a partnership that put DataRobot in the room earlier in the buying process, but nothing was in place — no engagement model, no delivery playbook, no pipeline.

Approach

I built the partnership from scratch. That meant designing an engagement model that worked for both organizations — clear on how work would be scoped, staffed, and priced when the two teams collaborated — and developing a delivery playbook that BCG consultants could actually use without requiring DataRobot support on every client interaction.

From there, I stood up the early client pipeline by working directly with BCG teams to identify where joint engagements made sense, building the relationships needed to move opportunities forward, and running the first engagements to prove out the model.

Impact

The first engagements generated $500K in revenue and opened a $2M pipeline. More importantly, the partnership established a repeatable motion — a defined engagement model and delivery playbook that didn't depend on individual relationships to scale.
