Pecan AI · Supply Chain GTM

250% YoY Revenue Growth via Supply Chain GTM Strategy

250% YoY revenue growth in target vertical
Situation

Pecan AI had strong predictive analytics capability but was selling horizontally without a focused vertical story. Supply chain was an underserved segment with clear demand for demand forecasting, inventory optimization, and supplier risk use cases — but no one had built the go-to-market motion to reach those buyers effectively.

The platform was technically capable of winning there; the gap was everything else.

Approach

I co-engineered the supply chain GTM strategy from scratch. That meant defining the ideal customer profile, developing supply chain-specific positioning and use case frameworks that resonated with ops and supply chain leadership rather than data teams, and building a delivery model that could scale beyond the first few wins.

I then led the early sales cycles — running discovery, shaping the value narrative, and closing enterprise customers who validated the segment thesis. Each early win refined the playbook and generated the proof points needed to accelerate the next one.

Impact
250%
YoY revenue growth in the supply chain vertical

The motion went from zero to one of the company's fastest-growing segments, with a repeatable playbook, reference customers, and a pipeline that no longer depended on individual relationships to source.
