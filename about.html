Bio

I help enterprises turn AI capability into something that shows up in their financials. The work has mostly been strategy and commercial execution — figuring out which AI bets are worth making and getting them to stick. I've worked across predictive, generative, and agentic AI, built partnerships with BCG and NVIDIA, and been part of programs delivering $100M+ in annual business impact. Lately I've been focused on the harder end of that problem: designing renewal and pricing models that make AI platforms survive past the first year.

I live on the North Shore of Massachusetts with my wife and daughter. Outside work I cook, make furniture, and ride gravel. I'm also slowly designing a house in rural Vermont, where I grew up — part workshop, part home base for being outdoors.
