$100M+ Annual Business Impact Across Enterprise Portfolio

$100M+ validated annual business impact · $2M+ GenAI pipeline
Situation

Managing a $20M+ enterprise portfolio spanning financial services, healthcare, CPG, and TMT, the challenge wasn't deployment — it was value. Customers had licensed the platform, but most struggled to connect AI capability to outcomes that showed up in the business.

Use cases were scattered, prioritization was unclear, and the path from model to measurable impact was rarely defined.

Approach

I led AI transformation strategy account by account — working directly with business and technical stakeholders to identify where AI would actually move the needle. That meant separating high-value bets from science projects, stress-testing feasibility against available data and organizational readiness, and building the business case for what to build first.

From there, I drove selected use cases through to production across predictive, generative, and agentic AI — managing the cross-functional work needed to go from proof of concept to something running in the business.

Impact
$100M+
validated annual business impact across the portfolio

The work spanned cost reduction, revenue uplift, and risk mitigation depending on the account and use case. In parallel, the GenAI expansion strategy created $2M+ in new pipeline — moving customers from initial platform adoption into the next generation of AI capability.
