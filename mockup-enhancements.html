DataRobot · Enterprise AI Transformation

Retention Operations Redesign and AI Tooling

30% improvement in in-period gross dollar retention
Situation

DataRobot was mid-transition from a traditional ML platform to a new consumption-aligned model. The product had changed significantly, but the renewal process hadn't kept pace. Renewal teams were operating without consistent playbooks, risk wasn't being surfaced until deals were already in trouble, and escalation paths were ad hoc.

As a result, gross dollar retention was under pressure at exactly the moment the business needed it most.

Approach

I rebuilt the renewal operating model from scratch — redesigning qualification criteria, creating tiered risk-scoring logic, and establishing clear escalation paths for a distributed global team.

In parallel, I built the internal tooling to run it: AI agents that automated follow-up workflows and surfaced account signals, and dashboards that gave leadership and individual reps real-time visibility into renewal status and risk concentration.

Impact
30%
improvement in in-period gross dollar retention

Renewal teams shifted from reactive to structured, operating with shared playbooks, consistent risk criteria, and tooling that replaced manual tracking. The operating model became the foundation for the global renewals function going forward.
